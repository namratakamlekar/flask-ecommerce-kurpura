Shipping & returns

How we deliver and what to do if something is wrong.

Please review and adjust these terms to match your actual policy.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 and above. For smaller orders a delivery charge of ₹49 applies. The charge is shown at checkout before you place your order.

Delivery time

Orders are packed in Pune and usually dispatched within 1–2 working days. Delivery time depends on your pincode.

Payment

We currently accept Cash on Delivery.

Damaged or wrong item

If your parcel arrives damaged or you receive a wrong item, email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace the item or refund you.

Returns

Because puja items such as camphor, dhoop and kumkum are consumable, opened packs cannot be returned. Unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Questions? Contact us.
