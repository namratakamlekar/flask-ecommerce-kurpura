What is Bhimseni karpura?

Bhimseni karpura is a premium, naturally derived camphor traditionally used in puja and aarti. You will find it in our Karpura category.

How should I store camphor?

Keep camphor in a tightly closed jar in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight so it stays fresh.
